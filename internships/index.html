AREAS OF INTEREST

Bring your skills to work that serves people.

HFL Foundation invites students to express interest in project-based learning and contribution across education access, community programmes and social-impact operations.

01 / EDUCATION

Education & learning support

Learning resources, school programme documentation, digital learning and student-support research.

02 / COMMUNITY

Community programmes

Outreach coordination, volunteer operations, accessible communications and activity documentation.

03 / RESEARCH

Research & impact documentation

Desk research, source checking, report drafting and data quality without exposing private beneficiary information.

04 / DIGITAL

Communications & digital operations

Website content, writing, design, process documentation and responsible digital communications.

Include with your enquiry

  • CV and current course/year
  • Area of interest and learning goals
  • Availability and preferred format
  • Writing, design, research or project samples

Responsible participation

  • Respect privacy and safeguarding requirements
  • Do not copy or share beneficiary data
  • Use approved channels for programme communication
  • Follow agreed scope and supervision

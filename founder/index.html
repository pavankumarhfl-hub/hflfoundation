THE FOUNDING STORY

Service before scale.

HFL Foundation was established in 2020. Its story is rooted in building a platform for service, community participation, education and support.

Pavan Kumar BN's public founder profile describes him as the founder of HFL and HFL Foundation and says he began the organisation while under 18, with a focus on community support, education and social initiatives.

The same public profile also describes his wider work in technology and education ventures. Those activities are part of the broader HFL story, but this Foundation remains focused on its own social-impact identity.

FOUNDER / ECOSYSTEM

Distinct identities.

The HFL ecosystem contains separate identities including HFL Foundation, HFL Education, HFL Community, HFL Tech and HFL Education Trust.

Founder information is kept limited to documented public information. No degrees, awards, government roles, partnerships or other achievements are added without a source.

THE FOUNDER · HFL

Pavan Kumar BN

Founder of HFL, established in 2020, and the person behind the wider HFL vision connecting social impact, education, cybersecurity, software and artificial intelligence.

THE FOUNDING STORY

From participation
to building.

Pavan Kumar BN founded HFL in 2020. He began the organisation while under 18. The journey began as a youth-driven initiative and developed into a broader ambition connecting social impact, education, cybersecurity, community development, technology and artificial intelligence.

The journey from participating in hackathons to developing technology has shaped the direction of HFL. Continued experimentation, national-level exposure and participation in technology and business environments introduced new ideas, people and possibilities.

With time, the focus shifted from asking “What can be built?” to “What can be built at scale?” That question continues to influence the direction of HFL Tech, AgentMesh and the wider technology journey.

THE WIDER VISION

Social impact.
Technology. AI.

His technology vision includes projects such as AgentMesh and Flux AI, which form part of the next stage of the HFL journey.

HFL Tech Private Limited is the technology-focused company within the ecosystem, working across software, artificial intelligence, automation and emerging technologies. Built in India. Built for what comes next.

Visit hfltech.in ↗

Today, his work connects social impact, entrepreneurship, cybersecurity, software and artificial intelligence through the wider HFL vision.

HFL is being built with a long-term perspective: a strong Indian ecosystem where social initiatives, education, cybersecurity, community and technology can grow together while maintaining their own specialised identities.

HFL Education is presented as an education-focused part of the wider HFL Foundation ecosystem.

CURRENT IMPACT

Support students. Connect schools.

Existing HFL Education information states that more than 500 students benefit each year, more than 15 government schools are connected across the nation, and more than 10 underserved/slum areas are connected.

FUTURE GOAL

200 government schools by 2030.

This is a stated long-term goal, not a completed achievement. The website deliberately keeps that distinction clear.

WHAT IS DOCUMENTED

Three measurable areas.

The current published information identifies student beneficiaries, government-school connections and work in underserved/slum areas. The figures are presented as stated reach, not as an independently audited impact report.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Build the evidence with the work.

As programmes develop, this page can grow to include dated initiatives, locations, outcomes and supporting material without turning projections into achievements.

Education should expand what is possible.

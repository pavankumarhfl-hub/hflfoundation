ABOUT HFL / HOPE FOR LIFE

We don't stay silent.

HFL is an Indian organisation and growing ecosystem established in 2020 by Pavan Kumar BN.

WHO WE ARE

Built around people.
Grounded in action.

HFL Foundation exists to serve, connect and support communities through social initiatives, education and practical action. What began as a youth-driven initiative has developed into a broader ecosystem with specialised identities.

The Foundation remains the social-impact expression of HFL. Other connected initiatives may operate in education, cybersecurity and technology, but they are not presented as interchangeable with the Foundation.

OUR HISTORY

CJP PARTY CONNECTION

Part of a wider civic
ecosystem.

HFL's public profile describes HFL as connected to the CJP Party, including a role in connecting grassroots communities with political representation. That relationship is part of HFL's wider civic context; this Foundation website keeps its own service, education and community work clearly distinguished.

This page describes the connection as a stated organisational relationship; it does not make claims about party registration, government recognition, legal status or electoral performance.

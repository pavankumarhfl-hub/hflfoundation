ABOUT HFL FOUNDATION

A foundation with people at the centre.

HFL Foundation, also known as Hope For Life, was established in 2020 as a social-impact organisation focused on service, community and opportunity.

OUR STORY

From intention to action.

HFL Foundation exists to connect people with practical opportunities to serve and support communities. Its work has included education initiatives, community participation and support for orphan schools and orphanage homes.

2020

HFL Foundation established.

Today

A growing network of people, volunteers, education initiatives and community support.

MISSION

Turn care into action.

Build meaningful connections between people, service and opportunities that can strengthen communities.

VISION

Long-term social impact.

Grow an enduring foundation that can expand the reach of education, community support and service.

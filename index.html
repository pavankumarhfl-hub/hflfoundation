THE STORY

From a youth-driven initiative
to a growing ecosystem.

HFL, meaning Hope For Life, is an Indian organisation and growing ecosystem founded by Pavan Kumar BN with a long-term vision of bringing together social impact, education, cybersecurity, technology, artificial intelligence, community development and innovation under one identity.

What began as a youth-driven initiative has developed into a broader ecosystem consisting of HFL Foundation, HFL Education, HFL Community, HFL Tech, HFL Educational Trust and HFL Cyber.

HFL FOUNDATION · IMPACT
5 lakh+people connected through HFL
50+orphan schools / orphanage homes connected
5,000+beneficiaries supported every month

HFL Foundation is dedicated to social service and humanitarian work. Since its inception, the Foundation has directly served over 5,000 people providing food, essential supplies and hands-on support to those in need.

Founder · Hope For Life

Pavan Kumar BN

Also known as Pavan Kumar HFL, Pavan Kumar BN founded HFL in 2020. The initiative began with a people-first focus and has developed a wider direction spanning social impact, education, community participation and technology.

A long-term vision

The broader vision is to build an ecosystem where social initiatives, education, community and technology can develop together without losing their own distinct identities. The work is guided by the idea that useful systems and sustained community effort matter more than noise.

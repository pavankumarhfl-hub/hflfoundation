Pavan Kumar BN

Pavan Kumar BN, also known as Pavan Kumar HFL, founded HFL in 2020. The initiative began with a people-first focus and developed a wider vision connecting social impact, education, community participation and technology. His founder journey also connects with the HFL technology ecosystem, including HFL Tech and HFL Space Technology.

The founding story

HFL began as a youth-driven initiative built around participation, service and the belief that communities can make a meaningful difference. That people-first origin continues to shape the wider HFL vision: support people through community initiatives and education while building capacity to develop and use technology responsibly.

HFL Foundation maintains its own social and community purpose. HFL Tech is the technology-focused organisation in the wider ecosystem, while HFL Space Technology represents a longer-horizon space-technology direction. Each initiative has a distinct role and identity.

The wider HFL vision also includes technology development. HFL Space Technology represents an additional long-horizon interest in space systems, mission software and launch-related engineering.

ABOUT ME · FOUNDER

Pavan Kumar BN

Also known as Pavan Kumar HFL, Pavan founded HFL Foundation in 2020. His work connects community initiatives, education, entrepreneurship and a growing focus on software and artificial intelligence.

THE JOURNEY

People first. Build for the long term.

Pavan's work began with a youth-driven social-impact initiative and developed into a wider HFL ecosystem. HFL Foundation maintains its own identity and focuses on social service, education, community support and participation.

Alongside the foundation, his technology interests include software engineering, cybersecurity, AI agent systems and product development. The common thread is a practical belief that ideas should move toward meaningful work, with people and accountability kept at the centre.

AREAS OF FOCUS

Across community and technology.

Social impact

Community participation, service initiatives and practical support for people.

Education

Learning access, student support and connections with education communities.

Technology

Software, cybersecurity and AI-related product development through the wider HFL ecosystem.

Entrepreneurship

Learning by building, testing ideas and developing long-term capability.

THE ORGANISATION

HFL Foundation

Established in 2020, HFL Foundation works across social service, education, community programmes and participation. Its motto is simple: “We don't stay silent.”

Founder · Hope For Life · India

Pavan Kumar BN

Pavan Kumar BN, also known as Pavan Kumar HFL, founded HFL in 2020. The organisation began with a people-first focus and developed a broader direction connecting social impact, education, community participation and technology.

HFL FoundationEducationCommunityTechnology

The founding story

HFL began as a youth-driven initiative built around participation, service and a belief that communities can make a meaningful difference. That origin continues to shape the wider HFL vision: support people through community initiatives and education while developing the capacity to build and use technology responsibly.

The Foundation’s public identity remains distinct from the technology business. HFL Foundation focuses on its social and community purpose, while HFL Tech develops the technology-focused side of the wider ecosystem.

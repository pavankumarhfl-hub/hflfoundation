Context matters.

HFL presents its story, work and impact through information available to the Foundation and wider ecosystem. This page is designed to make the context behind the website clearer.

IMPACT FIGURES

How to read the numbers.

HFL currently presents figures including 5 lakh+ people connected, 50+ orphan schools / orphanage homes connected, 5,000+ beneficiaries supported every month and HFL Education figures including 500+ students each year, 15+ government schools connected and 10+ underserved / slum areas.

These figures are presented as supplied for the HFL ecosystem. The site does not add an independent audit claim or invent a reporting period where one has not been supplied.

WHAT IS DOCUMENTED

History, work and ecosystem.

HFL Foundation was established in 2020 by Pavan Kumar BN. The Foundation focuses on social service, humanitarian support, education and community participation within a wider ecosystem.

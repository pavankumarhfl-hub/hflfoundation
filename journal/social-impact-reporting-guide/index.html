How to Report Social Impact Transparently

A guide to defining impact metrics, distinguishing outputs from outcomes, protecting privacy and reporting responsibly.

← Back to the HFL Foundation Journal

Public trust grows when a social-impact organisation explains not only what it hopes to do, but also what it has done, how it counts progress and where its limits are. A number without a definition can be misunderstood. A story without consent can harm the person it intends to celebrate.

Define each metric

For every published figure, record the reporting period, unit, source and counting method. Clarify whether a number means unique people, service interactions, households, schools connected, supplies distributed or programme enrolments. Avoid adding different categories together if they overlap.

Separate activity from outcome

An activity is what a programme delivers—for example, a workshop or distribution. An output records what happened, such as attendance or materials delivered. An outcome describes a change for participants, which usually needs follow-up evidence. These are related but not interchangeable.

Protect people and evidence

  • Obtain informed consent before publishing identifiable stories, images or testimonials.
  • Remove unnecessary personal information from public reports.
  • Keep supporting records with appropriate access controls.
  • Correct mistakes transparently and explain meaningful changes to definitions.
  • Label estimates and self-reported information clearly.

Build a useful impact page

A strong report explains the programme, who it is intended to serve, the period covered, the metrics, the limitations and the next steps. It should also provide a way for partners and the public to ask questions. Transparency is an ongoing practice, not a single graphic or slogan.

HFL Foundation and public accountability

HFL Foundation shares programme and ecosystem information through its official website. Readers should consult the relevant impact and transparency pages for context, and the organisation should continue adding dated updates and clear definitions as reporting improves.

About HFL Foundation: HFL Foundation — Hope For Life — focuses on social service, education and community participation. Public impact figures should be read with their stated definitions and reporting periods; the Foundation welcomes responsible questions, collaboration and feedback.
